Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Lumaby is a baby care companion for logging sleep, feeds, diapers, growth and milestones. This page explains what data Lumaby collects, why, and who it is shared with.

If you use Lumaby without an account

Everything you log is stored only in your browser on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you create an account

We store the following in our database, hosted by Supabase:

  • Account details: your email address and the name you enter. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not get access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Baby profile: the parent name, baby name, date of birth and emoji you enter.
  • Logs: for Premium members, the entries you log (for example feeds, sleep, growth measurements and notes) are synced so they are available on all your devices.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Premium, your plan and its renewal or end date.

Each account can only read its own data.

Payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the merchant of record. Your card details go directly to them; Lumaby never sees or stores them. Lemon Squeezy tells us your subscription status so we can turn Premium on or off.

Other services

  • Hosting: the app is served by Netlify, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site.
  • Fonts and libraries: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when your browser downloads them.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We don't use your data for anything other than running Lumaby.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, your browser shows notifications on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your browser settings.

Your choices

  • Export: you can download your logs as a CSV file from the app's settings.
  • Deletion: see Deleting your account below.
  • Subscriptions: you can cancel at any time through the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.

Deleting your account

In the app: tap the round picture at the top right of the home screen to open My Account, then Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. It happens straight away.

Without the app: email us from the address you signed up with at the contact address below, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Deleting removes your login and everything stored with it: your babies' logs, growth, milestones, appointments, reminder settings and notification registrations, and any partner access you shared. Entries you added to someone else's baby as a partner stay with that baby's owner. Nothing is kept afterwards, except that our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, keeps its own payment records as the law requires. If you have a Premium subscription that renews, cancel it first so you aren't charged again.

Children

Lumaby is meant to be used by parents and caregivers, not by children. The information about your baby is entered by you and is treated as part of your account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
